This is that mod: [MoanMod](https://github.com/IkariDevGIT/MDRGMoanMod), also posted on [the official itch.io page](https://itch.io/t/6004461/-moaning-mod-moaning-and-expressions-for-jun-by-ikaridev) with KlahTune's blessing.

It's a MelonLoader mod that adds moaning, breathing and facial expressions during sex scenes, tied to her pleasure/lust/sympathy. Clips cycle so it doesnt repeat the same moan over and over.  Everything is tweakable live and in-game via my good friend Echo's [Mod Settings Menu](https://github.com/Echo5Dev/MDRG-ModSettingsMenu).

The upcoming 2.0.0: settings moved fully into the Mod Settings Menu, moaning got gated on unlocking Advanced AI instead of finishing the prologue (I gen thought that was the same, test your shit Ikari), added a changelog popup, and decoupled moan volume from the game's sfx slider and so on yadayada (Will add a proper changelog once 2.0.0 comes out). 2.0.0 will also add a new tiering system, so you'll get different sounds based on factors such as her affection, how often she came in one session and so on.
